About the chapter

Who we are

Our mission

The IEEE Aerospace and Electronic Systems Society chapter at Manipal University Jaipur exists to give students a practical route into aerospace and electronic systems — the radar, navigation, avionics, and space technologies that most curricula only touch in theory.

We run hands-on workshops, host engineers and researchers from industry and ISRO, field teams for national design competitions, and support member-led special interest groups building real hardware and software.
